The rules (score worked out again on every GPS point)
  • Off route: 10 / 25 / 40 for 50 / 150 / 300+ m away, plus 2 per minute off (max 40)
  • Standing still: 3 per minute after 2 free minutes (max 25)
  • Loops: 15 per return to the same spot (max 30)
  • Left the destination's safe zone: 15
  • Taking too long: free until 1.5× the expected walk time, then 2 per minute (max 30)
30+ = check-in on the phone · 60+ = caregiver alert
